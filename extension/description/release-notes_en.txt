LibreCharts 4.0 - first public release.

Charts for LibreOffice Impress in the style of think-cell: column, bar, waterfall,
Marimekko, line, area, pie, scatter, butterfly and Gantt charts, drawn as native shapes
and fed from a Calc data sheet. Annotations, data links, slide elements (Harvey balls,
check marks, agenda) and label placement are included.
