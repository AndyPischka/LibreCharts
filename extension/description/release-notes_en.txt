LibreCharts 7.0 - the message on the slide, and the workflow around it.

New in 7.0: hide, rewrite or reset single labels; focus a chart on chosen series or categories;
add a source line and free callouts; set a per-chart font size and legend position. Manual value
axes, axis titles, a logarithmic axis and trendlines with forecasts, R2 and equations. Dumbbell,
bullet, Pareto, funnel and box plot charts, styled tables with Harvey balls, check marks,
sparklines and a heatmap, and 2x2 matrices. Distribute and align commands, process, chevron and
pyramid graphics, an icon set and chart templates. Import CSV, ODS and XLSX data, build a chart
from clipboard cells, keep data links up to date, audit a deck with jump-to-slide and export its
specification. A palette and font dialog, colour-blindness simulation, alternative text for every
shape and design checks over master slides and layouts.

Charts saved by 7.0 cannot be read by 6.0 or older; 7.0 opens every older presentation unchanged.
